Fluentiq.
At home on your iPhone.

The same practice workspace, theme and logo, with an icon on your Home Screen.

  1. Open Fluentiq in Safari

    Tap “Open Fluentiq” below. If you arrived through WhatsApp or another in-app browser, open the link in Safari first.

  2. Tap Share

    Open Safari’s Share menu, then find “Add to Home Screen.” It may be under “More” or “Edit Actions,” depending on your iOS version.

  3. Add Fluentiq

    Keep “Open as Web App” enabled if shown. Tap “Add,” then launch Fluentiq from its new Home Screen icon.
