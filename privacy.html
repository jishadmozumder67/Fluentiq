Your practice. Your data.

Privacy information for the Fluentiq browser edition · 11 November 2026

Default: saved on your device

Practice results, settings, drafts, profiles and the mistake notebook are stored in your browser. Browser storage and exported backup files are not an encrypted vault. Clearing site data can remove your progress. No advertising or analytics scripts are included.

Speaking and recordings

Microphone access is requested when you use a speaking or recording feature. Browser speech recognition may send speech to the browser vendor's recognition service. Availability and privacy depend on your browser.

Voice uploads for AI marking are off by default. If enabled and your own AI backend is configured, selected recording excerpts and your responses are sent to that endpoint for assessment. The supplied backend forwards them to Google's Gemini API. It does not write audio to its database; this does not guarantee deletion or non-retention by the upstream AI provider.

Optional AI feedback

No AI service is connected in the download. When you configure one, speaking transcripts, written answers, prompts and practice summaries may be sent there for feedback. Review that provider's current data-use terms before enabling it. Do not include sensitive information in practice responses.

Optional accounts

Without a backend, email profiles stay in this browser. With the supplied Cloudflare backend and D1 database, your email, display name, password verifier, session records and synced practice data are stored in your configured cloud account. Passwords are derived with PBKDF2 rather than stored in readable form. Guest mode is available without an email address.

Rate-limit counters can be stored using a daily hash of your network address. The backend does not intentionally log submitted answers or recordings. Cloudflare and the AI provider have their own operational logs and privacy terms.

Export or delete

Use Settings → Data & privacy to export a backup, clear local data or delete a configured account. Audio saved separately in your browser can require the recording deletion control or clearing site data. Delete any backup files you exported separately. The site operator must provide a real contact address before a public account service is launched.
